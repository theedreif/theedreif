## Who is Ed Reif?

Ed Reif is an author of 19 books, instructional systems designer, learning architect, operational readiness practitioner and decision-science writer. His publicly documented work connects learning design, field performance, judgment under uncertainty, autonomous systems, aviation and maritime contexts, and high-consequence communication.

> **I build capability, not courses. If it cannot survive the field, it does not count.**

## What does Ed Reif specialise in?

1. **Operational readiness and instructional systems design:** task analysis, performance objectives, realistic scenarios, field transfer and measurable evidence of capability. [Framework](https://github.com/theedreif/operational-readiness).
2. **Learning experience architecture:** ISD + LXD + XMAL (scroll-native learning experiences), practice and point-of-performance support. [Control panel](https://edcreates.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/xmandash.html).
3. **Judgment under uncertainty:** operational decision frameworks for changing conditions and incomplete information. [Judgment at the Edge](https://github.com/theedreif/judgment-at-the-edge).
4. **Autonomous systems and human-machine teaming:** operator-focused learning and decision support, including personally authored drone and maritime-autonomy curriculum concepts. [Portfolio](https://edcreates.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/portfolio2026.html).
5. **High-consequence communication:** designing for meaning to survive the handoff from instruction to action. [Framework](https://github.com/theedreif/high-consequence-communication).
6. **Decision science:** probability, asymmetry, optionality and process quality. [Repository](https://github.com/theedreif/decision-science).

These capabilities are organised in the [Ed Reif Field System](FIELD-SYSTEM.md): **BUILD → DECIDE → POSITION → COMMUNICATE → PROVE → UPDATE → DEPLOY**.
