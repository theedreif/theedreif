# Ed Reif

### Operational Readiness Architect · Author · Decision-Science Practitioner

**I build capability, not courses. If it cannot survive the field, it does not count.**

My work sits at the intersection of **operational readiness, judgment under uncertainty, decision science, autonomous systems, and field learning**.

I design learning and decision systems for the point where plans meet reality—when operators have incomplete information, limited time, changing conditions, and consequences attached to the decision.

## What I Work On

**Operational Readiness**  
Moving beyond course completion toward demonstrated capability: scenario-based learning, friction, telemetry, trust calibration, and evidence from performance.

**Judgment at the Edge**  
A practical operating model for decisions under pressure:

> **SIGNAL → CONDITION → LIMIT → DECISION → EVIDENCE**

**Decision Science**  
Asymmetry, probability, optionality, downside protection, behavioral economics, and making better decisions when outcomes cannot be guaranteed.

**Autonomous Systems**  
Human judgment around drones, uncrewed maritime systems, autonomy, operator readiness, and the changing relationship between people and machines.

**High-Consequence Communication**  
Designing communication for environments where the gap between *said, heard, understood,* and *done* can affect mission outcomes.

## Flagship Repositories

## Operating Principles

> **Scenario is the product.**  
> **Friction is the mechanism.**  
> **Telemetry over testing.**  
> **Trust must be calibrated.**  
> **Evidence beats completion.**

## From Experience to Doctrine

My work draws on instructional design, maritime operations, aviation and defense training, autonomous systems, international field experience, publishing, and decision science.

The recurring question is simple:

**What must a person be able to notice, decide, and do when reality departs from the plan?**
