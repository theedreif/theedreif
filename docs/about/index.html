About The Poker Lobotomy

The Poker Lobotomy is Ed Reif's poker decision-training system: 20-Second Rewires, 60-Second Rewires, the 500-Second Poker Rewire, and long-form Learn Poker in Your Sleep sessions designed to improve decisions under uncertainty.

Created by Ed Reif

Ed Reif is an author and decision-science practitioner whose work explores probability, asymmetry, judgment under uncertainty, and better decision-making.

Four learning modes

Short Attention Span Theater delivers 20-second rewires. 60-Second Rewires isolate one leak, one hand, one rule, and one better decision. The 500-Second Poker Rewire compresses 25 leaks into a two-part review. Learn Poker in Your Sleep provides long-form repetition and mental rehearsal.

Core topics

Texas Hold'em strategy, probability, GTO, bet sizing, position, pot odds, bluffing, multi-way pots, tilt control, bankroll discipline, and the mental game of poker.
