TRIPLE BARREL BLUFF

When Should You Fire the Third Barrel?

The river has to justify the bluff. Prior investment does not.

LEAKBetting the river because you already bluffed flop and turn.
RULEThe river card, your range, and villain’s range must still support the bluff.
BETTER DECISIONStop when the evidence stops supporting the bet.

The decision

A two-street bluff can become emotionally sticky. After betting flop and turn, the river starts to feel like the required ending. That is sunk-cost thinking, not poker strategy.

Re-evaluate every street. Ask whether the river changes range advantage, blocker effects, value density, or the opponent’s ability to fold. A failed two-street bluff does not automatically require a third barrel.
