POKER SHOWDOWN VALUE

Don’t Bluff Away Your Showdown Value

A medium-strength hand can already be good enough to win. Betting it does not automatically improve the decision.

LEAKTurning hands with natural showdown value into unnecessary bluffs.
RULEAsk what worse hands call and what better hands actually fold.
BETTER DECISIONCheck back more often when the hand can win naturally.

The decision

Players often feel pressure to finish the story with another bet. But if worse hands rarely call and better hands rarely fold, the bet can destroy value rather than create it. Separate value, bluff, and showdown before acting.

When your hand retains real showdown value, checking can preserve the pot, keep weaker holdings in range, and prevent you from polarizing a hand that never needed to become a bluff.

One leak. One rule. One better decision.
