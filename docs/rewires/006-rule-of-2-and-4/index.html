Poker Rule of 2 and 4: Estimate Draw Odds Fast

The Rule of 2 & 4

The Rule of 2 and 4 is a fast table estimate: with one card to come, multiply outs by about 2; with two cards to come, multiply outs by about 4.

By Ed Reif · The Poker Lobotomy

LEAKCalling draws by feel without translating outs into an approximate probability.
RULEOuts × 2 for one card to come; outs × 4 for two cards to come.
BETTER DECISIONEstimate draw probability, compare it with the price of the call, and include implied odds only when future payoff is realistic.

Why this matters

Exact combinatorics are useful away from the table, but a live decision often needs a fast approximation. The Rule of 2 and 4 turns a count of clean outs into a rough percentage you can compare with pot odds.

The word clean matters. An out that completes your draw but gives an opponent a better hand is not worth a full out. The shortcut improves decisions only when the inputs are honest.

Fast example

With a nine-out flush draw and one card to come, 9 × 2 suggests roughly an 18% chance to improve. With both turn and river available, 9 × 4 gives a rough 36% estimate. Compare that probability with the price you are being offered before calling.
