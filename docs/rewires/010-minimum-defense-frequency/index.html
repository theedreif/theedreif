Minimum Defense Frequency: MDF Without Auto-Calling

The MDF Reality Check

MDF estimates how often a range must continue to stop a bettor from profiting automatically with any two cards. It does not tell you that this specific hand must call.

By Ed Reif · The Poker Lobotomy

LEAKTurning Minimum Defense Frequency into an automatic calling rule.
RULETheory provides the baseline; evidence decides the exploit.
BETTER DECISIONUse MDF to understand pressure, then fold more against under-bluffers and defend appropriately against players who over-bluff.

Why this matters

MDF is useful because it shows how bet size changes the burden on the defender. Larger bets can profit with fewer folds, while smaller bets need more folds. But the concept operates at the range level, not as a command attached to every bluff catcher.

Real opponents deviate. Some populations under-bluff rivers; some players fire too often. A strong decision starts from the theoretical benchmark and then updates with credible evidence rather than worshipping the baseline.

Fast example

Your range may need to continue at a certain frequency against a bet, but that does not mean the bottom of your bluff-catching range is required to call against a player who almost never bluffs. Defend the right hands, against the right person.
