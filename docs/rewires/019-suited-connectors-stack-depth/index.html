Suited Connectors: Why Stack Depth Matters

Why Suited Connectors Need Deep Stacks

Suited connectors are not profitable because they are pretty. They need enough effective stack depth to get paid when they make straights, flushes, and strong disguised draws.

By Ed Reif · The Poker Lobotomy

LEAKCalling with suited connectors when stacks are too shallow to reward their low-frequency big outcomes.
RULEPretty cards still need economics.
BETTER DECISIONPlay suited connectors more selectively when effective stacks, position, and opponent tendencies provide real implied-odds upside.

Why this matters

Hands like 7♠6♠ often miss the flop or make marginal pairs. Their attraction comes from the ability to make less obvious strong hands that can win a large pot. If the remaining stacks are shallow, there may not be enough money behind to compensate for the misses.

Position improves the equation because it helps you realize equity and control pot size. Deep stacks increase upside, but they also increase the skill requirement because mistakes can become large.

Fast example

Calling several big blinds with suited connectors against a short stack leaves little future payoff even when you connect strongly. Against deeper effective stacks and an opponent who overpays one-pair hands, the same call can have a much more credible path to profit.
