Operational Readiness Scenario Design Checklist: 12 Essential Checks

A 12-point checklist for building exercises that reveal decisions, limits, handoffs, evidence and gaps.

By Ed Reif · 2026-11-16 · An educational framework; not a claim of independently validated results.

This checklist assesses the quality of a scenario design, not whether participants have already been validated. Use it before a tabletop, simulation, learning journey or supervised field exercise.

The 12 questions

  1. Mission: Is the task described as observable performance?
  2. Role: Is the performer and their decision authority clear?
  3. Conditions: Are system, environment and information constraints specified?
  4. Standard: Is success defined with verified or explicitly unverified thresholds?
  5. Signal: Is the relevant change detectable from the scenario information?
  6. Friction: Are constraints credible, relevant and proportionate?
  7. Limit: Are stop, escalation and safety boundaries unambiguous?
  8. Decision: Must the participant choose an action rather than recite a fact?
  9. Handoff: Can assessors observe whether communication was understood?
  10. Consequence: Does the scenario respond plausibly to choices?
  11. Evidence: Can decisions, actions, timings and recovery be recorded?
  12. Retest: Is there a process to address gaps and test changed conditions?

What a completed checklist means

It means the design includes key ingredients for useful observation; it does not mean a person, team or autonomous platform is operationally ready. Apply relevant safety policies and qualified performance assessment.
