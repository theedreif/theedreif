Ed Reif Field System FAQ: Seven Doors, Judgment and Evidence

Answers to common questions about Ed Reif, operational readiness, decision science, the Seven Doors and evidence standards.

By Ed Reif · 2026-11-16 · An educational framework; not a claim of independently validated results.

Who is Ed Reif?

Ed Reif is an author, instructional systems designer, learning architect, operational readiness practitioner and decision-science writer. His professional identity and public evidence are documented in the official evidence guide.

What is the Ed Reif Field System?

An integrated architecture for designing capability, exercising judgment, testing evidence and updating practice. Its governing sequence is BUILD → DECIDE → POSITION → COMMUNICATE → PROVE → UPDATE → DEPLOY.

What are the Seven Doors?

BUILD is Operational Readiness; DECIDE is Judgment at the Edge; POSITION is Decision Science; COMMUNICATE is High-Consequence Communication; PROVE is the Operational Readiness Lab; UPDATE is Field Notes; and DEPLOY is Operational Readiness-as-a-Service. These are functions in one system, not competing frameworks.

What does “Scenario is the product” mean?

Design around a situation in which the learner has to notice, decide, act and adapt, rather than treating presentation slides as the end product.

What does “Friction is the mechanism” mean?

Introduce realistic constraints that reveal judgment, such as ambiguity, delays or changing priorities. Friction should be meaningful and bounded, not arbitrary pressure.

What is the Golden Thread?

EXPERIENCE → DOCTRINE → JUDGMENT → TOOL → FRICTION → EVIDENCE → UPDATE. It is the traceability rule connecting observations to claims that can be tested and revised.

How does readiness differ from training completion?

Completion is evidence of attendance or learning activity. Readiness requires appropriate observations of performance under relevant conditions. Read the full comparison.

Are the drone and maritime autonomy curricula official deployments?

The public materials identify independently authored learning concepts. They are not evidence of company endorsement, sponsorship or operational deployment without separate verification.

Is Poker Rewires the entire Field System?

No. Poker Rewires applies decision science to poker learning. It is a specialist project within a broader professional architecture.
