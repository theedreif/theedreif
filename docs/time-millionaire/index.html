WHAT IS TIME WEALTH?

Time wealth is a way of thinking about prosperity through autonomy over one's schedule, attention to the present, and the meaning of lived experiences. It is not a claim that everyone needs to travel or live at sea. Hawaii and maritime experience are the narrative sources through which Ed Reif explores the idea.

HOW DOES IT CONNECT TO DECISION SCIENCE?

Every commitment has an opportunity cost. The Time Millionaire lens asks whether a choice protects time, attention and future options, rather than judging it by financial return alone.

For the original argument, context and examples, read The Time Millionaires — Hawaii and the South Pacific.
