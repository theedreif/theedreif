**Scope:** Actual AI answer mentions and source citations; **separate from SEO readiness** and from the presence of schema.org metadata, a sitemap or llms.txt.

**No cross-platform AI citation rate has been measured yet.** Do not infer an AI citation score from GitHub content quality, generic search results, metadata completeness or an AI's ability to open a public page.

## Prompts to test (use these verbatim)

1. **Identity:** Who is Ed Reif and what does he do?
2. **Expertise:** What is Ed Reif known for in operational readiness and instructional systems design?
3. **Framework:** What is the Ed Reif Field System and how are its seven domains organised?
4. **Evidence:** What public evidence supports Ed Reif's learning architecture and operational readiness work?
5. **Specialist project:** Who created Poker Rewires, and how does it relate to decision science?
6. **Applied projects:** Has Ed Reif published independent learning concepts about Skydio DFR or autonomous maritime systems? Are those official company projects?

## Test method

- Test each prompt in a **new, neutral session** on ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity, when accessible.
- Repeat each prompt **three times per platform**. Record exact model/version, date, geographic region, browsing/search setting and whether results were personalised or memory-aware.
- Save the complete answer, public citation URLs and screenshots or transcripts, and note whether the answer confuses authored portfolio concepts with verified employment, endorsements or deployments.
- Count a **citation** only when the AI answer provides a clickable source reference to an Ed Reif-owned page or a genuinely independent source about him; record the source type. A bare brand mention is a *mention*, not a citation.
- Record source quality, accuracy and mistaken identity separately. Search-engine ranking and technical SEO are not AI citation metrics.
- For a given platform and prompt set: **citation rate = responses with at least one qualifying citation / total responses tested**. Report sample size (n) beside every rate; list third-party citations separately from owned-source citations.
- Retest monthly after substantial updates, keeping the original test prompts fixed. Optionally test new prompts in a separately labelled cohort.
