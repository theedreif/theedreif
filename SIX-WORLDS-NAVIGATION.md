# Six Worlds Navigation Specification

**Version 1.0 — September 2026**

This file defines the shared public navigation pattern for the origin/life-story ecosystem surrounding the Ed Reif Field System.

It does **not** redefine the [Ed Reif Field System Manifest v1.0](FIELD-SYSTEM.md).

> **Six Worlds = ORIGIN · Seven Doors = OPERATION · One Field System = SYSTEM**

## The Six Worlds

1. **[EdReif.com](https://www.edreif.com/)** — canonical identity and synthesis hub.
2. **[Decision Science](https://www.thereisnolimitlikenolimit.com/)** — probability, poker, asymmetry, risk, and judgment under uncertainty.
3. **[Deja Blue](https://crusiewithed.blogspot.com/)** — maritime life, mobility, cross-cultural experience, and world travel.
4. **[Share Fair Isle](https://www.sharefairisle.com/)** — adaptation, constraint, place, attention, and remote-island life.
5. **[Rock & Roam](https://rockandroamingib.blogspot.com/)** — Gibraltar, geographic optionality, remote work, and portable output.
6. **[Skyelark MacDoglet](https://skyelarkdoglets.blogspot.com/)** — attention, companionship, cognition, belonging, and family narrative.

The Worlds document where experience and public evidence originated. They may bridge contextually to one or more operational Doors, but they do not own or map one-to-one to those Doors.

**EdReif.com · Decision Science · Deja Blue · Share Fair Isle · Rock & Roam · Skyelark MacDoglet**

The semantic footer content should be:

```text

EdReif.com · Decision Science · Deja Blue · Share Fair Isle · Rock & Roam · Skyelark MacDoglet

THE FIELD SYSTEM — OPERATION
BUILD · DECIDE · POSITION · COMMUNICATE · PROVE · UPDATE · DEPLOY

THE GOLDEN THREAD — TRACEABILITY
EXPERIENCE → DOCTRINE → JUDGMENT → TOOL → FRICTION → EVIDENCE → UPDATE
```

Each label must link to the canonical destination above.

## Contextual Bridges

A World may additionally expose a context-relevant Door, for example:

- Decision Science → **POSITION**
- Rock & Roam → **POSITION**
- Skyelark MacDoglet → **UPDATE** where Field Notes is relevant
- Deja Blue → **COMMUNICATE** or **BUILD** where a specific maritime story supports those domains
- Share Fair Isle → the Field System or Source Architecture when no single Door owns the relationship
- EdReif.com → the complete Field System

These are bridges, not ownership mappings.

## Acceptance Test

A public World passes navigation integrity when:

1. a visitor can reach all six Worlds;
2. a visitor can reach the canonical Field System;
3. the Seven Doors appear in canonical order;
4. Worlds are described as origin and Doors as operation;
5. the Golden Thread uses the canonical Field System sequence;
6. no local loop is presented as the complete Field System;
7. all links resolve to the canonical destinations.

> **One canonical architecture. Many paths to it.**
