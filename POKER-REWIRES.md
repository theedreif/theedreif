## What is Poker Rewires?

Poker Rewires is a specialist decision-training project that translates common Texas Hold'em mistakes into short, actionable learning experiences. It uses poker as a laboratory for probability, risk, emotional regulation, decision quality and judgment under uncertainty.

**Learning rule:** *One leak. One rule. One better decision. Next hand.*

The Poker Lobotomy is the creative learning system behind the Poker Rewires formats. **Poker Rewires is one specialist application of Ed Reif's wider Field System — it is not the full professional portfolio.**

## Why it belongs in the Field System

Poker Rewires demonstrates a transfer-oriented instructional pattern: **notice the leak → identify the rule → rehearse the decision → apply it on the next hand**. It connects [Decision Science](https://github.com/theedreif/decision-science) with [Operational Readiness](https://github.com/theedreif/operational-readiness) and the [Learning Journey Architect](https://github.com/theedreif/field-notes/tree/main/skills/learning-journey-architect) approach.
