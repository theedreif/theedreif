# Broke Living, High Rolling

## The Unauthorized Biography of Jean-Robert Bellande

**Author:** Ed Reif  
**Format:** Book / unauthorized biography  
**Subject:** Jean-Robert Bellande, professional poker player

*What if the richest life in poker belonged to a man who was constantly broke?*

This independently authored biography explores Bellande's public poker persona, high-stakes lifestyle, and the tension between financial swings and a life measured in experiences. It is a narrative biography, not an official or endorsed account.

**Every poker legend has a story. The cards are only the beginning.**

Behind the million-dollar pots, World Series bracelets, legendary bluffs, and unforgettable rivalries are extraordinary people who turned a game of chance into a lifelong pursuit of skill, fortune, and identity.

*Legends of Poker* is a compelling biography series exploring the lives of the players who shaped poker history—from the smoky backrooms of Las Vegas to the highest-stakes tables in the world.

These are stories of ambition and obsession, spectacular victories and devastating losses, unlikely friendships, bitter rivalries, reinvention, and redemption.

Some came for the excitement. Others chased the money. A few became legends because they discovered something far more valuable: a genuine love for the game.

Through unauthorized biographies, the series goes beyond tournament results and televised poker hands to explore the personalities, decisions, risks, and defining moments behind poker's most fascinating figures.

Because poker has never been just about winning money. It's about reading people, embracing uncertainty, taking calculated risks, and discovering who you really are when everything is on the line.
